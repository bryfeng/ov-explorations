Exploration · hero · Cobalt, reversed

Meaning in, fingerprint out

A neutral, content-blind root of trust.

Independent systems can share one record of what happened and in what order, without adopting each other's rules.

An event stays readable in its own system. What crosses into the record is a fingerprint of it, in a position. In every figure, the dashed line is what is proposed.

What it says

Content-blindness, drawn: the record never receives the event, only h(e). Order is the position where it lands. The read is the same method run again by someone holding the disclosed event; a match is inclusion, not validity.

What it must not say

The method inside h(·) is the system's. The source says commitment; it does not say zero-knowledge, Merkle or anything else. The boundary is drawn; the method is not named. Values are illustrative.

Against Cobalt

Cobalt resolves noise into types; this dissolves types into noise on purpose. Same density field, same vertical boundary, opposite direction. The record's beam replaces Relay's event list.
